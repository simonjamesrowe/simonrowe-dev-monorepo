## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Relevant AI Blog Titles (Priority: P1)

As a reader, I want AI-generated blog posts to have descriptive titles based on their themes rather than generic "This Week in AI" or "AI & Tech Roundup" titles, so that I can decide if the content is relevant to me before clicking.

**Why this priority**: Titles are the primary way users discover content. Generic titles reduce engagement and make the site look repetitive.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by generating a new AI digest blog and observing that the title reflects the specific articles summarized.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a set of tech articles about Java and Spring Boot, **When** the weekly digest is generated, **Then** the blog title reflects "Java and Spring Boot" themes rather than a generic "This week in AI".

---

### User Story 2 - Retrospective Title and Image Fix (Priority: P1)

As a site owner, I want all historically generated AI blogs to have their titles fixed and featured images regenerated based on the newly improved prompts, so that my past content looks professional and engaging.

**Why this priority**: Existing generic content degrades the overall quality of the site.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by running the application with the new Mongock migration and verifying existing digest blogs have updated titles and images.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a database with historical blogs titled "AI & Tech Roundup" or containing "This week in AI", **When** the application starts and runs migrations, **Then** those blogs are updated with new titles based on their content, and their featured images are regenerated.

### Edge Cases

- What happens if the AI fails to generate a good title during migration? (Fallback logic)
- How does the system handle image generation failures during migration? (Keep the old image or retry)

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: System MUST ensure future AI-generated digest blogs avoid generic phrases like "this week in AI" and instead use theme-based titles. (Note: Existing logic in `DigestMetadataGenerator` may already handle this, but it must be verified/improved).
- **FR-002**: System MUST include a Mongock migration to identify existing digest blogs with generic titles.
- **FR-003**: The migration MUST generate new, theme-based titles for identified historical blogs using the AI service.
- **FR-004**: The migration MUST regenerate the featured image for these historical blogs based on their new titles and content.
- **FR-005**: System MUST NOT alter manually authored blogs (only AI-generated digests).

### Key Entities

- **Blog**: Represents the blog post entity, specifically those generated by the `WeeklyDigestAgent`.
- **Mongock Migration (ChangeUnit)**: Represents the migration script that updates the `Blog` entities.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: 100% of historically generated digest blogs have their titles updated to remove generic "this week in AI" phrasing.
- **SC-002**: 100% of historically generated digest blogs have new featured images.
- **SC-003**: New digest blogs are verified to generate theme-specific titles consistently.

## Assumptions

- We can identify AI-generated digest blogs by looking for specific tags (e.g., "Weekly Digest") or checking the author/origin flag.
- The AI service is available and stable enough to process multiple title and image regeneration requests during a database migration.
- Existing logic in `DigestMetadataGenerator` can be reused or slightly adjusted for the migration's title generation.
