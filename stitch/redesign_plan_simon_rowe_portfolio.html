# Portfolio Redesign Project: Simon Rowe ## Overview Redesigning the personal portfolio of Simon Rowe, a Software Engineering Leader. The goal is to create a modern, AI-native aesthetic that works seamlessly across desktop and mobile, while also providing a robust admin console for content management. ## Target Audience - Potential employers and recruiters - Fellow software engineers and tech enthusiasts - Readers of the technical blog ## Key User Flows & Screens ### 1. Public Portfolio (Visitor Experience) * **Landing / Home:** A high-impact hero section with an AI-integrated search/chat, professional summary, and quick links (CV, Socials). * **Experience & Skills:** A detailed view of professional history and technical expertise, with interactive elements. * **Blog / Content Hub:** A clean, readable list of latest posts with categories and search functionality. * **Contact / Get in Touch:** A streamlined contact form and direct contact details. ### 2. Admin Console (Owner Experience) * **Dashboard:** High-level overview of site analytics, recent blog performance, and quick actions. * **Content Manager (Blog/Skills/Experience):** A unified interface to add, edit, or remove entries for the portfolio sections. * **Settings & Profile:** Manage personal details, CV uploads, and site-wide configuration. ## Design Direction - **Style:** Clean, professional, yet modern and "tech-forward." - **Palette:** Drawing inspiration from the current dark hero and teal accents, but refined for better contrast and accessibility. - **Typography:** Modern sans-serif for high readability on technical content. - **Interactions:** Subtle animations and a focus on the AI-powered search experience.
