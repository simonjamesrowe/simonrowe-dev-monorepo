## Three short films of the real app

Each film runs the real CoParent, its Spring Boot backend and the real model, on a local copy of the
stack. The family is synthetic: Alex Morgan and Sam Taylor, with Mia and Leo. Nothing in these
films belongs to a real family, and no email was sent while making them. The narration is a
synthetic voice.
